The NineSQL code is licensed under Apache 2.0. As provided in its section 6, this license **grants no rights** to the "NineSQL" name or logo.

## Allowed without asking
- Saying that your product or service "uses NineSQL", "is compatible with NineSQL" or "is based on NineSQL"
- Using the name in articles, tutorials, talks or training materials
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their name, including in distribution repositories

## Requires written permission
- Distributing a **modified** version under the "NineSQL" name (a fork must change its name; distribution packaging patches are fine)

For any request, open a discussion on the repository.
